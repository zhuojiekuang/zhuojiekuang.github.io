- [CS 70 (Discrete Math and Probability Theory)](https://su24.eecs70.org/)
  - Logic, infinity, and induction; applications include undecidability and stable marriage problem. Modular arithmetic and GCDs; applications include primality testing and cryptography. Polynomials; examples include error correcting codes and interpolation. Probability including sample spaces, independence, random variables, law of large numbers; examples include load balancing, existence arguments, Bayesian inference.
  - UCS 1 in Summer 2024
- [Computer Science Mentors (CSM)](https://csmentors.studentorg.berkeley.edu/)
  - CSM is a student-run organization that provides guidance and resources in a smaller classroom environment through group tutoring sessions. Each section has 4-6 students and focuses on material from one of the courses listed above.
  - Mentor for CS 70 in Fall 2023
